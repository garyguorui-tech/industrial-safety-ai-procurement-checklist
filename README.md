# Industrial Safety AI Procurement Checklists & Buyer Diligence Guides

Open, evidence-verified procurement checklists and technical diligence frameworks for industrial safety AI, field sensing hardware, edge alerts and robotics integration.

## Published Guides

- [Multi-Site Industrial Safety AI Procurement Checklist](guides/multisite-industrial-safety-ai-procurement-checklist.md) — Field sensing, edge alerts, robotics responsibility split (`Robot Action Brain` vs. third-party OEM carriers), entity-separated public records, and a test-permit walkthrough.
- [Due Diligence on Physical AI in Industrial Sensing & Safety](guides/physical-ai-industrial-sensing-due-diligence.md) — Separating demonstrated product modules (`PanoGuard`, `Robot Action Brain`, 3D point-cloud comparison, `Building X` / `WeEnergy` / `WeConnect`) from per-site customization, entity-verified Tier 1 filings (7 independent source chains), and explicit commercial unknowns.

- [Mobile All-View Panoramic Terminals vs. PTZ Control Balls in Industrial Safety](guides/mobile-panoramic-vs-ptz-control-ball.md) — Comparing solid-state `360° × 360°` multi-fisheye terminals (`PanoGuard` SKUs) against motorized PTZ domes across weight, FOV, weak-network resilience, hazardous-area/gas-sensing workflow fit, and multimodal safety AI (with strict entity & safety boundaries).

- [Confined-Space & Hot-Work Safety AI](guides/confined-space-multimodal-safety-permit-workflow.md) — Coordinating fixed corridor CCTV, mobile `360°` all-view terminals (`PanoGuard` SKUs), wireless gas-sensing information, acoustic leak sensing, and work-permit context to cut false alarms and run a five-step test-permit acceptance protocol.

- [3D Laser Point-Cloud vs. BIM Model Comparison in Construction](guides/construction-3d-point-cloud-bim-deviation-guide.md) — Separating as-built geometry checks from safety monitoring, defining per-site survey calibration and `Building X` O&M handover boundaries, and classifying Tianjin 117 case materials transparently.

## Verification & Scope Principles

- **Primary source separation**: Government notices, standards-library records, awards programme listings and vendor-published project overviews are cited separately by legal entity and proof boundary.
- **Safety boundary**: Field monitoring, gas/acoustic sensing and on-device AI notifications support human supervision and response traceability; they do not replace statutory safety devices, DCS/process interlocks, permit-to-work approvals or human supervision.
- **Editorial workflow**: New guides are prepared and reviewed via Draft Pull Requests before merging to `main`.
